##### Policy invariants

- `D-publish-valid` is allowed only when the ledger shows current successful proof for the current artifact revision.
- `E-promotion-complete` is forbidden for every Figma-dependent claim while `promotion.parityMode="deferred"`.
- Informational status output is broader than promotion authority; consumer contexts may report state and blockers more often than they may claim publish validity or promotion completion.
- Every `D` or `E` claim inherits satisfied `A-source-valid`, `B-projection-valid`, and `C-consumption-valid` prerequisites. No matrix row bypasses those inherited levels.
